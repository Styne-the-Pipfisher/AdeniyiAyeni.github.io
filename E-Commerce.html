Nykaa E-Commerce Performance Analysis

Business Problem

Nykaa needed visibility into product performance, discount effectiveness, and customer purchasing behavior to optimize revenue and profitability.

Data Overview

  • Sales transactions
  • Product categories
  • Discount rates
  • Customer behavior

Analysis Approach

  • Data cleaning and transformation
  • KPI development (Revenue, Profit, AOV)
  • Category performance analysis
  • Discount impact evaluation

Key Insights

  • High discounts did not always lead to higher profit
  • Few categories contributed majority of revenue
  • Some products consistently underperformed despite high sales volume

Recommendations

  • Optimize discount strategy by category
  • Focus marketing on high-margin products
  • Re-evaluate underperforming SKUs
