Key Insights

  • High discounts did not always translate into higher profitability.
  • A small number of product categories generated a large share of revenue.
  • Some products generated strong sales volume but weak profit margins.
  • Customer purchasing behavior revealed opportunities for targeted promotions.
  • Revenue concentration highlighted critical categories driving business performance.

Recommendations

  • Optimize discount strategies by category.
  • Prioritize high-margin products in marketing campaigns.
  • Review underperforming products for pricing or inventory adjustments.
  • Develop customer segmentation strategies for personalized promotions.
  • Monitor profitability alongside revenue when evaluating performance.
