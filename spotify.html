Spotify Listening Behavior Analysis

Data Analytics Case Study | Power BI Dashboard

📌 Problem Statement

Spotify wanted to understand how users interact with music on the platform — specifically: why users skip songs, when engagement is highest, and what type of content keeps users listening longer.

The business problem was a lack of clarity around user engagement behavior, which affected recommendation quality and content strategy.

🎯 Objective

To analyze user listening behavior and identify patterns that influence engagement, retention, and song completion rates.

📊 Data Used

  • Listening history dataset
  • Track names and artists
  • Play duration
  • Skip events
  • Listening timestamps

🔍 Analysis Approach

  • Cleaned and structured raw listening data
  • Created KPIs: skip rate, completion rate, total listens
  • Grouped songs by genre, artist, and time of day
  • Built Power BI dashboard for visualization

💡 Key Insights

  • Afrobeat songs had the lowest skip rate, showing higher engagement
  • Peak listening hours were between 7PM – 11PM
  • Short-duration tracks had higher completion rates
  • New or unfamiliar artists had significantly higher skip rates

🧠 Problem Solved

The analysis helped identify what drives user engagement and what causes users to skip content. This directly addresses the challenge of improving recommendation accuracy and user retention.

🚀 Outcome / Achievement

  • Improved understanding of user listening behavior patterns
  • Identified key drivers of engagement and skip behavior
  • Provided insights that can improve recommendation algorithms
  • Enabled data-driven decisions for content and playlist optimization
